## 📖 About The Concept

**Dadi's Heritage Rasoi** is a specialized culinary concept designed to bring back the lost, authentic flavor of homemade Indian pickles (*achaar*) that are slow-matured under natural sunlight for 18–25 days in genuine ceramic *martabans* (barnis). 

Unlike commercial supermarket pickles that rely heavily on synthetic vinegar (acetic acid), refined palm oils, and chemical stabilizers, Dadi's Heritage pickles are preserved strictly using traditional methods:
1. **Cold-pressed organic mustard oil (*kacchi ghani*)**
2. **Himalayan rock salt & natural sunlight**
3. **Whole stone-ground roasted spices**

## ⚡ Hyper-Local Express Delivery Model

To maintain optimal freshness and prevent long warehouse storage:
- **Cloud Kitchen Hub:** Operates from a central kitchen hub serving a strict **8 km radius**.
- **Delivery Timeline:** Prepared, sealed in glass jars with induction foil seals, and dispatched via on-demand local delivery partners (**Dunzo, Porter, Shadowfax**) within **45–60 minutes** directly to customers' dining tables.
- **Interactive Delivery Radius Checker:** Built-in address/pincode verification tool for local residents.
