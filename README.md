## Brand & Product Overview

**Venu's Kitchen** specializes in authentic, traditional Indian pickles (*achaar*) crafted strictly using domestic culinary practices:
* **Cold-Pressed Mustard & Til Oil (Kacchi Ghani)**
* **Natural Sun Curing & Himalayan Rock Salt**
* **100% Food-Grade Glass Jars with Induction Foil Seals**
* **Zero Synthetic Vinegar (Acetic Acid) or Chemical Stabilizers**

## Hyper-Local 45–60 Minute Delivery Model

* **Operating Hub:** Dedicated 8 km radius servicing designated residential colonies and sectors.
* **Dispatch Timeline:** Packed fresh from maturation batches, sealed, and handed over to on-demand point-to-point delivery partners (Dunzo, Porter, Shadowfax) within 45–60 minutes.
* **Delivery Checker:** Built-in verification tool for local sector and pincode validation.
