## 🏛️ Architecture & Two-Page Structure

As requested, the user experience is separated into two dedicated, beautifully animated, and ultra mobile-responsive pages:

### 1. The Hero Landing Page (`index.html`)
* **Spacious, High-Impact Header:** Official branding, active navigation, and direct kitchen contact.
* **Appetizing Hero Section:** Bold typography, 21-day sun-curing story, and high-definition jar photography with floating live badges (*Batch #12 in Cellar*, *Hyper-Local Fleet Dispatch*).
* **Interactive Locality Radius Checker:** Instant verification for local sectors/pincodes with 45–60 minute ETA confirmation.
* **Signature Varieties Preview:** Top 3 bestsellers with a direct CTA button leading to the full store.
* **Hyper-Local Logistics Partner Fleet:** Explaining the 8 km radius cloud kitchen dispatch model (Dunzo, Porter, Shadowfax).
* **Traditional Sun Curing vs Factory Pickles:** Why genuine mustard oil and rock salt are superior to commercial synthetic vinegar.
* **Customer Testimonials & Foodie Reviews:** Real neighbourhood tasting feedback.

### 2. The Dedicated Store Page (`store.html`)
* **Full Pantry Catalog:** All 7 authentic homemade pickle varieties in sterilized glass jars.
* **Interactive Live Search & Filter Bar:** Instant keyword search and category filtering (*All Pickles*, *Mango Specialties*, *Chilli & Garlic*, *Zero-Oil Lemon*, *Mixed Vegetables*).
* **Product Quick-View Modals:** Complete ingredient lists, base oils, shelf-life, and traditional recipe lore.
* **Unified Cart Drawer:** Synchronized via `cart.js` with real-time item counter, quantity increments, and live total.
* **Disabled Checkout Button with Official Sign:** Clearly visible but gated during the pre-launch phase, accompanied by a direct WhatsApp advance ordering button.
