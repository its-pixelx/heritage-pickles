Operational Logistics

Local Kitchen Delivery Radius & Fleet Standards

To ensure zero leakage, peak freshness, and preserve the delicate pungency of cold-pressed oils, Venu's Kitchen operates strictly on a point-to-point local delivery model.

1. Induction Sealed Glass

Every jar features an airtight foil induction seal under the metal cap to ensure leak-proof handling during transport and preserve the authentic aroma.

2. 45–60 Minute Express Dispatch

Orders within our 8 km kitchen circle are assigned to direct hyperlocal courier riders (Dunzo, Porter, Shadowfax) for immediate doorstep handover.

3. Clean Room Packing

Jars are filled in an inspected, hygienic kitchen environment using sterilized utensils and food-grade glass martabans.

Hygiene & Safety Protocols

Commitment to Authentic, Chemical-Free Preservation

Commercial pickles manufactured in factories often substitute traditional mustard oil with cheaper refined palm oil and add synthetic acetic acid (chemical vinegar) to force rapid fermentation.

At Venu's Kitchen, we adhere strictly to the centuries-old Indian sun-maturation method:

  • Pure cold-pressed mustard and sesame oils (Kacchi Ghani).
  • Hand-pounded spices (Fenugreek, Fennel, Kalonji, Turmeric).
  • Zero artificial food colors, zero synthetic preservatives.
  • Natural shelf life of 12 months preserved with rock salt and oil.
