Why Factory Pickles Cannot Match Venu's Kitchen

Commercial pickle manufacturers boil vegetables in synthetic acetic acid (chemical vinegar) and use cheap refined palm oil to mass-produce jars in hours. At Venu's Kitchen, we refuse shortcuts. We follow the authentic 21-day rooftop sun-maturation process using pure cold-pressed mustard oil and rock salt.

Pure Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil (Kacchi Ghani) Preserves authentic pungency and natural crunch without artificial chemicals.
21-Day Sun-Curing in Ceramic Martabans Enzymes develop naturally under sunlight, producing deep flavor that improves with age.
100% Food-Grade Glass Jars Never stored or shipped in plastic buckets. Non-reactive, hygienic, and eco-friendly.
